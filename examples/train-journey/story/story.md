# The Evening Train

Maya and her seven-year-old daughter Lila are taking the evening train to visit
Lila's grandmother, Nani, whom Lila has not seen since she was very small.

The little train curls through the hills at sunset while a flock of birds wheels
over the fields.

Inside the carriage, Maya leads Lila by the hand down the aisle. The conductor,
an older railway worker in a navy cap, tips the cap to them.
"Evening! The window seats are free," the conductor says.
Lila hurries to the window seat and Maya sits down beside her.

Lila gazes up at the window, watching the hills slide by. Then she turns to her
mother. "Mama, will Nani remember me?"

Maya smiles. "She talks about you every single day."

Lila leans her head on Maya's shoulder as the light fades, and the train rolls on
into the night.
